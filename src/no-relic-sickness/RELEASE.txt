NoRelicSickness
===============

Stops the Relic from hurting V. The Relic, its storyline and its scenes are
unchanged -- only the sickness effect is removed.

Requires
--------
  * Cyberpunk 2077 (base game; the Phantom Liberty record is included and is
    simply unused if you do not own the expansion)
  * RED4ext
  * TweakXL

Install
-------
Copy the r6 folder into your Cyberpunk 2077 directory:

    <Cyberpunk 2077>/r6/tweaks/NoRelicSickness.yaml

That is the whole mod. No game file is modified, so nothing needs a backup and
there is no deploy step.

Uninstall
---------
Delete the file:

    <Cyberpunk 2077>/r6/tweaks/NoRelicSickness.yaml

What it changes
---------------
Six shipped TweakDB status-effect records are overridden. Every record keeps its
name and its inheritance edge, so all quest call sites still resolve:

    BaseStatusEffect.JohnnySicknessAbstract      drops the CameraAnimation tag
    BaseStatusEffect.JohnnySicknessLow           clears VFX and packages
    BaseStatusEffect.JohnnySicknessMedium        clears VFX and packages
    BaseStatusEffect.JohnnySicknessMediumQuest   clears VFX and packages
    BaseStatusEffect.JohnnySicknessHeavy         clears VFX and packages
    BaseStatusEffect.q304_songbird_relic_shock   clears VFX and packages (PL)

Removed: the fullscreen yellow/glitch overlay, the camera-breathing animation,
the fullscreen SFX, and the stamina-regen penalty.

Not changed: the Relic, its perk tree, its quests and its scenes. Scripted beats
where V visibly collapses are story animation and stay.

Compatibility
-------------
This is a TweakDB overlay, so it composes with mods that touch quests or scenes.
If another mod also overrides these same records, load order decides the winner.
